Reflective Writing

Insights from my first-year academic journey

Adapting to University Life: My First-Year Experience

Enrolling in university education is truly an unforgettable milestone for me. The transition from high school with its strict rules to university with its freedom was not too easy for me.

The first hurdle I faced was the management of my time. Originally, I did not understand how much time I would need for readings and assignments and, as a result, began submitting rushed work. It made me turn to planning and scheduling my time wisely.

Moreover, gaining experience in university made me realize that a growth mindset is essential for success. The perception of the difficulty of certain technical issues changed as I learned that the feeling of confusion is a necessary part of the learning process.

Taking some time to write a reflection portfolio is indeed a good idea. Now I have come to the conclusion that studying skills can be developed.

As for my goals for the remaining year, I want to keep on studying regularly, seeking feedback earlier, and improving my technical and business communication skills as well.

Learning Through Collaboration: Reflections on Group Work

Our group was formed early in the semester when the lecturer assigned us randomly for a collaborative task.

Forming

The first meeting was polite but cautious. Most of us did not know one another well. We introduced ourselves, exchanged contact details, and clarified the assignment requirements.

Storming

Differences in working styles appeared when we began dividing tasks. Some members preferred independent work while others wanted frequent check-ins. A brief disagreement arose over the quality of early contributions. We addressed this by setting clear deadlines, creating a shared folder, and agreeing on regular short updates.

Norming

Once roles and communication expectations were clear, the group settled into a productive routine. We held brief progress discussions and supported one another when someone fell behind.

Performing

In the later stages the team worked effectively. Tasks were completed on time, members offered help freely, and the final output was stronger than any individual effort would have been.

Adjourning

After submission we held a short reflection. We acknowledged what went well and noted areas for future improvement. The experience ended with mutual appreciation and a clear sense of closure.

Key Lessons

I learned that conflict is normal in groups and can be productive when addressed early. Clear communication, agreed norms, and flexibility are essential. I also recognised that I sometimes hesitate to raise concerns; in future I will address issues sooner. Overall, the experience strengthened my understanding of teamwork and collaborative problem-solving.
